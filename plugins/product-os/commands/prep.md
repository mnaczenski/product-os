---
description: Prepare a meeting or 1:1 from workspace artifacts.
argument-hint: <meeting or person> [topic]
---
If strategy/ or templates/ does not exist in this workspace, stop and tell the user to run /workspace-setup in an empty product-os repository first.

Input: $ARGUMENTS

Use recent work/digests/ for what changed in the relevant artifacts.
For a meeting: goal, what to decide, relevant ideas, initiatives, decisions and risks, 3 questions to ask.
For a 1:1 with a direct report: their open ideas and initiatives, status vs metrics, open decisions, upcoming risks, and coaching questions about the work.

Hard rule: work only from artifacts and outcomes. No scoring, ranking or performance judgments of the person. Performance conversations are the leader's job, not the agent's.
