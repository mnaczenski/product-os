---
description: Close the feedback loop: draft a reply to the person or team who raised a request.
argument-hint: <signal id, problem id, or request description>
---
If strategy/ or templates/ does not exist in this workspace, stop and tell the user to run /workspace-setup in an empty product-os repository first.

Input: $ARGUMENTS

1. Find the request, related problem, idea and decisions.
2. Determine the honest status: planned, being explored, not now, will not do.
3. Delegate to the writer subagent: a short, clear reply with the reason. "Will not do" is a valid answer and must include why.
4. Note on the signal that the loop was closed, with date.
